The Wizarding Wire

The Wizarding Wire is a full-stack web application inspired by the beloved (by myself, and millions) book series, Harry Potter.

I have leveraged the MERN stack, utilizing TypeScript based on the Model-View-Controller (MVC) design pattern to create a simple, but thorough, CRUD app.

Check out The Wizarding Wire on Github!

Tello Drone Mission

To experiment with Robotics and Artifical Intellignece, I utilized a DJI Tello Drone and Google's MediaPipe open source framework to develop a drone mission control application.

I enhanced the drone’s vision by adding object detection, utilized threading to process commands coming from my own computer, and learned how to create a video writer to save the mission recordings.

Check out Tello Drone Mission on Github!
